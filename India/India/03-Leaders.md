## Study Notes

- Nehru → longest formative influence (Non-Alignment, mixed economy, institutions).
- Indira → centralization, wars, Emergency.
- Rao + Manmohan → economic turning point (1991).
- Vajpayee → nuclear + attempted peace with Pakistan.
- Modi era → current affairs focus (Article 370, foreign policy activism).
