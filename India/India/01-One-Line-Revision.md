# India — One-Line Revision (Must Remember)

**High-yield for CSS (especially shared history + bilateral)**

---

### Independence & Early Years

- **Independence of India**: 15 August 1947
- **First Prime Minister**: Jawaharlal Nehru (15 Aug 1947 – 27 May 1964)
- **First President**: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (1950–1962)
- **Republic Day / Constitution enforced**: 26 January 1950
- **Death of Mahatma Gandhi**: 30 January 1948
- **Death of Nehru**: 27 May 1964
- **Lal Bahadur Shastri becomes PM**: 1964 (dies 11 Jan 1966 in Tashkent)

### Wars & Agreements with Pakistan

- **First Kashmir War**: 1947–1948 → UN Ceasefire Line
- **Indus Waters Treaty**: 19 September 1960
- **1965 War**: Aug–Sep 1965 → Tashkent Declaration (10 Jan 1966)
- **1971 War**: 3–16 Dec 1971 → Bangladesh created
- **Simla Agreement**: 2 July 1972
- **Nuclear tests (Pokhran-II)**: May 1998
- **Lahore Declaration**: 21 February 1999
- **Kargil Conflict**: May–July 1999

### Key Leaders

- **Indira Gandhi**: PM 1966–1977 & 1980–1984 (assassinated 31 Oct 1984)
- **Rajiv Gandhi**: PM 1984–1989 (assassinated 1991)
- **P.V. Narasimha Rao**: 1991–1996 (economic liberalization)
- **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**: 1998–2004 (nuclear tests, Lahore bus, Kargil)
- **Manmohan Singh**: 2004–2014
- **Narendra Modi**: 2014–present

### Other High-Frequency

- **Non-Aligned Movement**: Founded under Nehru’s leadership
- **Emergency**: 25 June 1975 – 21 March 1977
- **Economic Liberalization**: 1991 (under Rao + Manmohan Singh as FM)
- **Article 370 abrogation**: 5 August 2019 (Jammu & Kashmir)
