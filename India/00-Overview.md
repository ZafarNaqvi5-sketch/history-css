## Historical Periods (Exam-relevant)

1. **Ancient & Medieval** (Indus Valley → Delhi Sultanate → Mughals) — shared with Pakistan
2. **British Period** (1757–1947) — Company rule → Crown rule → Independence movement
3. **Partition & Independence** (1947)
4. **Nehruvian Era** (1947–1964) — Nation-building, Non-Alignment, mixed economy
5. **Indira Gandhi Era** (1966–1977, 1980–1984) — Wars, Emergency, centralization
6. **Post-Emergency to Liberalization** (1977–1991)
7. **Economic Reforms & Rise** (1991–present)
8. **Contemporary India** (Modi era focus for current affairs)
