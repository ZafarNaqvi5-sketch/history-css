## Relations with Pakistan — Core Features

### Why “All-Weather”?
- Strategic convergence against India
- No major bilateral disputes
- Consistent support across different Pakistani governments
- Economic + defence + diplomatic pillars

### Key Pillars

1. **Strategic / Defence**
   - Border agreement 1963
   - Defence equipment and joint production
   - Diplomatic backing on Kashmir

2. **Economic**
   - CPEC (roads, energy, Gwadar port, special economic zones)
   - Growing trade and investment
   - Part of larger BRI

3. **Diplomatic**
   - Coordination in multilateral forums
   - Mutual support on core interests

### Points of Caution / Debate
- Debt sustainability of CPEC projects
- Security of Chinese personnel and projects in Pakistan
- Balancing China’s expectations with Pakistan’s other relationships (US, Gulf, etc.)
