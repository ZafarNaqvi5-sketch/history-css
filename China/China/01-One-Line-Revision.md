# China — One-Line Revision

---

- **PRC founded**: 1 October 1949
- **Sino-Indian War**: 1962
- **China–Pakistan Boundary Agreement**: 2 March 1963
- **Karakoram Highway**: Construction 1960s–1978 (opened 1978)
- **Deng Xiaoping reforms begin**: 1978
- **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)**: Announced 2013
- **CPEC formally launched / major push**: 2015 onward
- **“All-Weather Strategic Cooperative Partnership”**: Current designation of China–Pakistan ties

### High-yield for Pakistan

- China is Pakistan’s most reliable strategic partner
- 1963 border agreement settled the boundary (Pakistan side of Kashmir region)
- CPEC is the flagship project of BRI in Pakistan
- Strong defence cooperation (including JF-17 joint production)
- Consistent diplomatic support on Kashmir in international forums
