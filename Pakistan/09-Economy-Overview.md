# Pakistan — Economy Overview (CSS High-Yield)

---

## Key Features & Patterns

- Agriculture still important (cotton, wheat, rice, sugarcane) but industry & services growing
- Repeated cycles: Crisis → Reform / IMF programme → Temporary recovery → New crisis
- High population growth + energy shortages + political instability = structural challenges
- Remittances (especially from Gulf) are a major support
- CPEC as major infrastructure & energy push since mid-2010s

---

## Historical Economic Phases

| Period              | Character                                      | Notes |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------|-------|
| 1950s–1960s        | Ayub era growth, Green Revolution beginnings  | Relatively high growth |
| 1970s              | Nationalization under Bhutto                  | Major industries taken over by state |
| 1980s              | Partial liberalization under Zia              | — |
| 1990s              | Structural adjustment, privatization attempts | Political instability hurt continuity |
| 2000s              | Musharraf-era growth + post-9/11 inflows      | — |
| 2008–present       | Repeated balance-of-payments pressure, IMF programmes | Energy crises, floods, political uncertainty |

---

## Must-Remember Points

- **Nationalization** (1972–77) under Bhutto — banks, large industries
- Later **privatization** efforts in 1990s and 2000s
- Multiple **IMF programmes** (frequent user of Fund facilities)
- **CPEC**: energy projects + infrastructure + Gwadar (strategic + economic)
- Major shocks: 2000s energy crisis, 2010 & 2022 floods, commodity price spikes
- Exports: textiles remain dominant; need for diversification often discussed
