# Pakistan — Wars & Major Conflicts

**Focus:** High-yield for CSS/PMS (Pakistan Affairs + Current Affairs)

---

## 1. First Kashmir War (1947–1948)

| Item              | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Duration**      | Oct 1947 – 1 Jan 1949 |
| **Trigger**       | Tribal invasion of Kashmir after Partition; Maharaja Hari Singh’s accession to India |
| **Main Theatre**  | Jammu & Kashmir |
| **Outcome**       | UN-brokered ceasefire (1 Jan 1949). Ceasefire Line established (later LoC). Pakistan controls Azad Kashmir + Gilgit-Baltistan; India controls Jammu, Valley, Ladakh. Plebiscite promised but never held. |
| **Significance**  | Origin of the Kashmir dispute; still unresolved. |

---

## 2. Indo-Pak War 1965

| Item              | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Duration**      | Aug – Sep 1965 (full war ~17 days) |
| **Trigger**       | Pakistan’s Operation Gibraltar (infiltrators into Kashmir) + Rann of Kutch earlier |
| **Key Events**    | Operation Grand Slam; India crosses international border (6 Sep – Defence Day); tank battles (Asal Uttar etc.) |
| **Outcome**       | Military stalemate. UN ceasefire. **Tashkent Declaration** (10 Jan 1966) – both sides withdraw to pre-war positions. |
| **Significance**  | Showed limitations of military solution; Soviet mediation role. |

---

## 3. 1971 War (Bangladesh Liberation / Fall of Dhaka)

| Item              | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Duration**      | Full war: 3–16 Dec 1971 (13 days); crisis from March 1971 |
| **Trigger**       | 1970 elections (Awami League majority), political deadlock, Operation Searchlight (25 Mar 1971), Indian intervention |
| **Main Theatre**  | East Pakistan (primary) + Western front |
| **Outcome**       | Pakistani forces surrender in Dhaka (16 Dec 1971). Bangladesh created. ~93,000 POWs. |
| **Post-war**      | **Simla Agreement** (2 Jul 1972) |
| **Significance**  | Greatest military and political trauma for Pakistan; loss of East wing; shift in civil-military dynamics. |

---

## 4. Kargil Conflict (1999)

| Item              | Detail |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Duration**      | May – Jul 1999 |
| **Trigger**       | Pakistani troops/militants occupy heights on Indian side of LoC in Kargil-Dras-Batalik sector |
| **Outcome**       | India launches Operation Vijay; recaptures positions. Pakistan withdraws under international pressure. |
| **Context**       | First major conflict after both countries became nuclear powers (1998). Came after Lahore Declaration (Feb 1999). |
| **Significance**  | Diplomatic isolation for Pakistan; domestic political fallout leading to Musharraf coup (Oct 1999). |

## Study Tips

- Always link war → treaty/agreement → long-term consequence.
- For exams: causes, course (key operations), outcome, and diplomatic settlement.
- Casualty figures and exact territorial claims are often disputed — treat carefully and note sources.
