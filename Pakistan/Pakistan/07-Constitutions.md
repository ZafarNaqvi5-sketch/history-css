# Pakistan — Constitutional History

---

## Overview of Constitutions

| Constitution       | Enforced          | System                  | Fate |
|--------------------|-------------------|-------------------------|------|
| Provisional (GoI Act 1935 adapted) | 1947–1956 | Dominion                | Replaced |
| **1956**           | 23 March 1956    | Parliamentary, Islamic Republic | Abrogated 7 Oct 1958 |
| **1962**           | 8 June 1962      | Presidential            | Abrogated 1969 |
| Interim (1972)     | 1972             | —                       | Temporary |
| **1973** (current) | 14 Aug 1973      | Parliamentary, Federal  | Still in force (heavily amended) |

---

## 1973 Constitution — Key Features

- **Adopted**: 10 April 1973 (unanimous)
- **Enforced**: 14 August 1973
- **Form**: Federal Parliamentary Republic
- **Legislature**: Bicameral (National Assembly + Senate)
- **State Religion**: Islam (Article 2)
- **Objectives Resolution**: Made substantive part via 8th Amendment (Article 2A)
- **Fundamental Rights**: Articles 8–28
- **Principles of Policy**: Articles 29–40
- **Islamic Provisions**: Articles 227–231
- **National Language**: Urdu (Article 251)
- **High Treason**: Article 6 (abrogating/subverting Constitution)

### High-Frequency Articles for Exams
- Art 1 → Federation & territories
- Art 2 → Islam as state religion
- Art 2A → Objectives Resolution
- Art 6 → High treason
- Art 8–28 → Fundamental Rights
- Art 25 → Equality of citizens
- Art 25A → Right to education
- Art 58(2)(b) → (historical) President’s power to dissolve NA (removed by 18th)

## Major Amendments (Must Know)

| Amendment | Year | Key Impact |
|-----------|------|------------|
| **8th**   | 1985 | Zia era. Made Objectives Resolution substantive (Art 2A). Gave President power to dissolve National Assembly (58-2-b). Validated martial law actions. |
| **13th**  | 1997 | Nawaz Sharif. Removed President’s power to dissolve NA and appoint service chiefs without PM advice. Restored parliamentary supremacy. |
| **17th**  | 2003 | Musharraf. Restored some presidential powers; validated LFO 2002. |
| **18th**  | 2010 | Largest reform. Removed 58(2)(b). Major devolution of powers to provinces. Strengthened parliamentary system. Abolished Concurrent List (mostly). |
| **25th**  | 2018 | FATA merger into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. |

## Constitutional Timeline (Quick)

- 1949 → Objectives Resolution
- 1956 → First Constitution
- 1958 → Abrogated (Martial Law)
- 1962 → Second Constitution
- 1969 → Abrogated
- 1973 → Current Constitution
- 1977 → Suspended by Zia
- 1985 → Restored with 8th Amendment
- 1997 → 13th Amendment (parliamentary restoration)
- 2003 → 17th Amendment
- 2010 → 18th Amendment (biggest civilian reform)
- 2018 → 25th Amendment (FATA)

## Study Focus

1. Why 1973 is considered the most legitimate (unanimous adoption).
2. How military regimes used amendments to legitimize power (8th & 17th).
3. How civilian governments tried to restore parliamentary balance (13th & 18th).
4. Federalism evolution → especially 18th Amendment impact on provinces.
