## Study Notes

- **Non-consecutive terms** are common (Benazir, Nawaz, Zardari).
- Always note **route to power** (election, appointment, coup, succession).
- For exams: focus on major policy shifts under each major leader (Ayub, Bhutto, Zia, Musharraf, post-2008).
