## 1947–1958: Creation & Early Instability

| Year / Date          | Event                                      | Significance |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------|--------------|
| 14 Aug 1947         | Independence of Pakistan                   | Dominion status under British Crown |
| Aug–Sep 1947        | Partition violence & mass migration        | ~10–15 million displaced; large casualties |
| Oct 1947            | First Kashmir conflict begins              | Tribal invasion + Indian troops |
| 11 Sep 1948         | Death of Muhammad Ali Jinnah               | Leadership vacuum |
| 12 Mar 1949         | Objectives Resolution passed               | Ideological foundation for future constitutions |
| 16 Oct 1951         | Liaquat Ali Khan assassinated              | First PM killed |
| 1954                | First Constituent Assembly dissolved       | Constitutional crisis (Ghulam Muhammad) |
| 23 Mar 1956         | First Constitution enforced                | Islamic Republic of Pakistan |
| 7 Oct 1958          | Martial Law declared                       | Ayub Khan takes power |

| 25 Mar 1971         | Operation Searchlight                      | Military action in East Pakistan |
| 16 Dec 1971         | Fall of Dhaka                              | Bangladesh independence; Pakistan loses East wing |
