# Pakistan — Geography & Strategic Locations

---

## Physical Features (High-Yield)

| Feature                  | Significance |
|--------------------------|--------------|
| **Indus River System**   | Lifeline of Pakistan; agriculture, water politics with India |
| **Himalayas / Karakoram / Hindu Kush** | Northern barrier; Siachen, strategic passes |
| **Balochistan plateau**  | Largest province by area; Gwadar, mineral potential, security issues |
| **Thar Desert**          | Southeast |
| **Coastline**            | Arabian Sea; Gwadar & Karachi ports |

---

## Strategic Locations & Chokepoints

| Location                | Why Important |
|-------------------------|---------------|
| **Gwadar Port**         | CPEC flagship; access to Arabian Sea / Indian Ocean |
| **Karakoram Highway**   | Land link to China |
| **Khyber Pass**         | Historic invasion / trade route toward Afghanistan |
| **Bolan Pass**          | Strategic route in Balochistan |
| **Siachen Glacier**     | Highest battlefield; control of heights |
| **Sir Creek**           | Disputed estuary with India (maritime boundary implications) |
| **Durand Line**         | 2,600+ km border with Afghanistan |
| **Line of Control (LoC)** | Kashmir divide with India |

---

## Administrative

- 4 provinces: Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan
- Islamabad Capital Territory
- Gilgit-Baltistan & Azad Jammu & Kashmir (special status)
- Former FATA merged into KP via 25th Amendment (2018)

---

## Strategic Chains (for answers)

- Indus River → Agriculture → Food security → Water sharing with India (Indus Waters Treaty)
- Gwadar → CPEC → China partnership → Indian Ocean access
- Western border → Afghanistan → Militancy / refugees / strategic depth debate
- Northern areas → China border → Karakoram Highway → strategic depth toward Central Asia
