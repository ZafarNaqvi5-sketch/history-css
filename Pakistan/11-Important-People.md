# Pakistan — Important People (CSS Priority)

Focus on political, military, constitutional, and movement leaders.

## Pakistan Movement & Founding

| Person                      | Significance |
|-----------------------------|--------------|
| **Muhammad Ali Jinnah**     | Quaid-e-Azam; founder; first Governor-General |
| **Allama Iqbal**            | Allahabad Address 1930; philosophical foundation |
| **Liaquat Ali Khan**        | First Prime Minister |
| **Fatima Jinnah**           | Sister of Quaid; political figure |
| **Chaudhry Rehmat Ali**     | Coined “Pakistan” |

## Post-Independence Political Leaders

| Person                      | Key Role / Period |
|-----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Ayub Khan**               | First military ruler (1958–69) |
| **Zulfikar Ali Bhutto**     | 1971–77; 1973 Constitution; nuclear programme start |
| **Zia-ul-Haq**              | 1977–88; Islamization; Afghan Jihad |
| **Benazir Bhutto**          | First woman PM (1988–90, 1993–96) |
| **Nawaz Sharif**            | Three-time PM; nuclear tests 1998 |
| **Pervez Musharraf**        | 1999–2008; post-9/11 alignment |
| **Asif Ali Zardari**        | President 2008–13 & 2024– |
| **Imran Khan**              | PM 2018–22 |
