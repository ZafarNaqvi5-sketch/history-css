# Pakistan — One-Line Revision (Must Remember)

**Purpose:** 5–10 minute rapid revision  
**Rule:** Dates are conventional exam dates. Always cross-check primary sources.

---

### Foundational

- **Lahore Resolution**: 23 March 1940 (session 22–24 March)
- **Independence of Pakistan**: 14 August 1947
- **First Governor-General**: Muhammad Ali Jinnah (14 Aug 1947 – 11 Sep 1948)
- **First Prime Minister**: Liaquat Ali Khan (15 Aug 1947 – 16 Oct 1951)
- **Death of Quaid-e-Azam**: 11 September 1948
- **Objectives Resolution**: 12 March 1949
- **Assassination of Liaquat Ali Khan**: 16 October 1951

### Constitutions & Martial Law

- **First Constitution**: 23 March 1956 (Islamic Republic declared)
- **First Martial Law**: 7 October 1958 (Ayub Khan)
- **Second Constitution**: 8 June 1962 (Presidential system)
- **Ayub resigns / Yahya takes over**: 25 March 1969
- **1970 General Elections**: 7 December 1970
- **Fall of Dhaka / Bangladesh independence**: 16 December 1971
- **Simla Agreement**: 2 July 1972
- **1973 Constitution adopted**: 10 April 1973 (enforced 14 August 1973)
- **Zia Martial Law**: 5 July 1977
- **Zulfikar Ali Bhutto hanged**: 4 April 1979
- **Zia dies in plane crash**: 17 August 1988

### Later Key Dates

- **Nuclear tests (Chagai-I)**: 28 May 1998
- **Lahore Declaration**: 21 February 1999
- **Musharraf Coup**: 12 October 1999
- **18th Amendment**: April 2010 (major devolution)
- **25th Amendment (FATA merger)**: May 2018
