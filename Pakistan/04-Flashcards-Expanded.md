### Dates (Advanced)

**Q:** When was the Objectives Resolution passed?  
**A:** 12 March 1949

**Q:** Date of first Martial Law in Pakistan?  
**A:** 7 October 1958

**Q:** When was the 1973 Constitution adopted and when enforced?  
**A:** Adopted 10 April 1973; enforced 14 August 1973

**Q:** Fall of Dhaka date?  
**A:** 16 December 1971

**Q:** Simla Agreement date?  
**A:** 2 July 1972

**Q:** Zia imposed Martial Law on?  
**A:** 5 July 1977

**Q:** Pakistan’s nuclear tests (Chagai-I)?  
**A:** 28 May 1998

**Q:** Musharraf coup date?  
**A:** 12 October 1999

**Q:** 18th Amendment year?  
**A:** 2010

**Q:** 25th Amendment (FATA merger) year?  
**A:** 2018

**Q:** Indus Waters Treaty signed?  
**A:** 19 September 1960

**Q:** Tashkent Declaration?  
**A:** 10 January 1966

**Q:** Lahore Declaration?  
**A:** 21 February 1999

### Leaders & Offices

**Q:** First Governor-General of Pakistan?  
**A:** Muhammad Ali Jinnah

**Q:** First Prime Minister?  
**A:** Liaquat Ali Khan

**Q:** Who was President during the 1971 war?  
**A:** Yahya Khan

**Q:** Who gave Pakistan its current (1973) Constitution?  
**A:** Zulfikar Ali Bhutto’s government

**Q:** Which amendment introduced Article 58(2)(b)?  
**A:** 8th Amendment (1985)

**Q:** Which amendment removed the President’s power to dissolve the National Assembly?  
**A:** 13th Amendment (1997) — later affected by 17th, restored by 18th

---

### Wars & Treaties

**Q:** What was the main outcome of the 1971 war for Pakistan?  
**A:** Loss of East Pakistan → creation of Bangladesh

**Q:** Which agreement turned the Ceasefire Line into the Line of Control?  
**A:** Simla Agreement (1972)

**Q:** Which rivers were allocated to Pakistan under Indus Waters Treaty?  
**A:** Western rivers — Indus, Jhelum, Chenab

**Q:** Name the post-1965 war agreement.  
**A:** Tashkent Declaration

---

### Concepts

**Q:** What is the Durand Line?  
**A:** 1893 border agreement between British India and Afghanistan; still disputed by Afghan side

**Q:** What does CPEC stand for?  
**A:** China–Pakistan Economic Corridor

**Q:** What was the Objectives Resolution?  
**A:** 1949 resolution that became the ideological basis of future constitutions (sovereignty belongs to Allah, delegated to the people within Islamic limits)
