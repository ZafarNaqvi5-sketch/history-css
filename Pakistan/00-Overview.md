## Historical Periods (Exam-friendly)

1. **Creation & Early Years** (1947–1958)  
   Dominion → Islamic Republic, political instability, first constitution.

2. **Ayub Khan Era** (1958–1969)  
   First martial law, 1962 Constitution, 1965 war, economic growth + inequalities.

3. **Yahya Khan & 1971** (1969–1971)  
   Elections, crisis in East Pakistan, war, creation of Bangladesh.

4. **Bhutto Era** (1971–1977)  
   Civilian rule, 1973 Constitution, nationalization, nuclear program start.

5. **Zia-ul-Haq Era** (1977–1988)  
   Martial law, Islamization, Afghan jihad, nuclear development.

6. **Democratic Decade** (1988–1999)  
   Benazir & Nawaz alternating, instability, nuclear tests 1998.

7. **Musharraf Era** (1999–2008)  
   Coup, 9/11 alliance, economic reforms, judicial crisis.

8. **Post-2008 Democratic Period** (2008–present)  
   18th Amendment, FATA merger, political polarization, economic challenges.
