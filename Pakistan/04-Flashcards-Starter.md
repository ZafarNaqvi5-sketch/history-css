### Dates

**Q:** When did Pakistan become independent?  
**A:** 14 August 1947

**Q:** Date of Objectives Resolution?  
**A:** 12 March 1949

**Q:** When was the first Constitution of Pakistan enforced?  
**A:** 23 March 1956

**Q:** Date of first Martial Law?  
**A:** 7 October 1958

**Q:** When was the 1973 Constitution adopted?  
**A:** 10 April 1973 (enforced 14 August 1973)

**Q:** Fall of Dhaka / Bangladesh independence?  
**A:** 16 December 1971

**Q:** Zia-ul-Haq imposed Martial Law on?  
**A:** 5 July 1977

**Q:** Pakistan conducted nuclear tests on?  
**A:** 28 May 1998 (Chagai-I)

**Q:** Musharraf coup date?  
**A:** 12 October 1999

**Q:** 18th Amendment year?  
**A:** 2010

**Q:** 25th Amendment (FATA merger) year?  
**A:** 2018

**Q:** First Governor-General of Pakistan?  
**A:** Muhammad Ali Jinnah

**A:** Liaquat Ali Khan

**Q:** Who imposed the first Martial Law?  
**A:** Ayub Khan (7 Oct 1958)

**A:** Yahya Khan

**A:** Zulfikar Ali Bhutto government

### Concepts / Events

**Q:** What was the Objectives Resolution?  
**A:** 1949 resolution that became the ideological foundation; sovereignty belongs to Allah, to be exercised by the people through elected representatives within Islamic limits.

**Q:** What was the main result of the 1970 elections?  
**A:** Awami League (East Pakistan) won overall majority → political crisis → 1971 war.

**Q:** Name the agreement after the 1965 war.  
**A:** Tashkent Declaration (10 January 1966)

**Q:** Name the agreement after the 1971 war.  
**A:** Simla Agreement (2 July 1972)
