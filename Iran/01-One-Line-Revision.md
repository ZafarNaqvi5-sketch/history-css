# Iran — One-Line Revision

---

- **Islamic Revolution**: 1979 (Shah overthrown; Ayatollah Khomeini returns)
- **Iran–Iraq War**: 1980–1988
- **Pakistan recognized Islamic Republic**: One of the first countries (1979)
- **JCPOA (Nuclear Deal)**: 2015 (US withdrew 2018)
- **Strait of Hormuz**: Critical chokepoint controlled/influenced by Iran

### Pakistan-related

- Iran was the **first country** to recognize Pakistan in 1947
- Both were members of CENTO (pre-1979)
- Iran supported Pakistan in 1965 and 1971 wars
- Post-1979: Generally stable but cautious relations
- Shared concerns over Baloch militancy on both sides of the border
- Occasional border incidents (e.g., Jan 2024 tit-for-tat strikes) followed by de-escalation
