## Relations with Pakistan — Core Points

**Positive / Cooperative factors**
- Early recognition and support in wars (1965, 1971)
- Shared interest in containing Baloch separatism
- Energy cooperation potential (IP gas pipeline idea)
- Both non-Arab Muslim countries with independent foreign policies

**Friction points**
- Sectarian dimension (Sunni–Shia)
- Competition for influence in Afghanistan
- Border security (militant groups operating across border)
- Differing alignments (Pakistan’s ties with Saudi/Gulf vs Iran’s rivalry with them)

**Overall character**: Generally stable and pragmatic neighbourly relations, punctuated by occasional security incidents that both sides prefer to contain quickly.

---

**CSS angle**: Iran is important as a neighbour, energy player, and part of the broader West Asian strategic environment that affects Pakistan.
