## Historical Periods (Exam-friendly)

1. **Durrani Empire & 19th century** — Buffer between British India and Russia
2. **Durand Line (1893)** — British–Afghan agreement; lasting dispute
3. **Monarchy to Republic** (until 1973/1978)
4. **Saur Revolution & Soviet Invasion (1979–1989)**
5. **Civil War & Taliban rise (1990s)**
6. **US-led intervention (2001–2021)**
7. **Taliban return (Aug 2021–present)**

---

## Core Themes for Pakistan / CSS

- Durand Line legitimacy & recognition issue
- Refugee flows into Pakistan
- Cross-border militancy (TTP etc.)
- Pakistan’s role in Afghan Jihad (1980s)
- Post-2021 recognition dilemma and border management
- China’s growing interest; India’s past influence
- Water (Kabul River) potential issues
