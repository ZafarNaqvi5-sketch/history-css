## Relations with Pakistan — Core Issues

1. **Durand Line** — Pakistan considers it international border; successive Afghan governments (including Taliban) have not formally accepted it.
2. **Refugees** — Millions of Afghans lived in Pakistan for decades.
3. **Cross-border militancy** — Especially TTP using Afghan soil (major current friction).
4. **Strategic depth / influence** — Historical Pakistani interest in friendly government in Kabul.
5. **Trade & connectivity** — Potential transit routes, but security problems limit it.
6. **India factor** — Competition for influence in Afghanistan.

### Phases of Relationship

- **1947–1979**: Cool to tense (Durand, Pashtunistan issue)
- **1979–1989**: Close cooperation against Soviets
- **1990s**: Pakistan support to Taliban
- **2001–2021**: Complex — official cooperation with US-backed government + accusations of dual policy
- **2021–present**: Initial hope → growing friction over TTP and border
