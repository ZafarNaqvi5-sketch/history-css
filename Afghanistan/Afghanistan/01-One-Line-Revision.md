# Afghanistan — One-Line Revision

---

- **Durand Line**: 1893 (British India–Afghanistan agreement; still disputed by Afghan side)
- **Soviet Invasion**: 25/27 December 1979
- **Geneva Accords**: 1988 (Soviet withdrawal framework)
- **Soviet withdrawal completed**: 1989
- **Taliban first capture Kabul**: 1996
- **US-led intervention begins**: 7 October 2001
- **Taliban fall**: November–December 2001
- **Bonn Agreement**: December 2001
- **US/NATO withdrawal & Taliban return**: 15 August 2021

### Pakistan-related

- Afghanistan was the only country that initially voted against Pakistan’s UN membership (later withdrew objection)
- Pakistan supported Afghan Mujahideen in 1980s
- Pakistan was one of only three countries to recognize Taliban government (1996–2001)
- Post-2021: Relations strained mainly over TTP and border management
